How much does AI automation cost for a small business in Idaho?

At Teton Peaks AI, plans start at $147 a month plus $497 setup. You can try missed-call text back first with a $297 pilot for 30 days, and an AI receptionist plan is $497 a month plus $2,497 setup. Prices are the same in Idaho Falls, Rexburg, Pocatello, Driggs, and every other town we serve.

What does each plan cost?

Missed-Call Rescue is $147 a month plus $497 setup, with up to 500 texts a month. Lead Engine is $297 a month plus $997 setup, with up to 1,500 texts a month. AI Front Desk is $497 a month plus $2,497 setup, with up to 2,000 texts a month.

Each of these three plans includes the one before it. Missed-Call Rescue texts back missed calls and alerts you. Lead Engine adds fast replies to web and Facebook leads, a booking link, and review requests. AI Front Desk adds an AI receptionist that answers, asks the right questions, and books 24/7.

Front Desk Lite is $347 a month plus $1,497 setup, with up to 250 texts a month. It's an AI receptionist that answers calls, takes messages, and sends you summaries. It doesn't connect to your booking calendar.

What happens if I go over my text limit?

Your texts keep sending. At 80% of your plan's monthly text limit, we let you know. On plans below AI Front Desk, we also offer an upgrade. There is no overage bill.

Can I try it before I commit?

Yes. The Missed-Call Rescue Pilot is $297 one-time for 30 days, with no monthly fee from us, plus carrier texting fees at cost. If it doesn't win back at least one missed-call lead in 30 days, you get your $297 back. Texting registration is paid at cost and isn't refunded.

If you keep going within 14 days, the full $297 counts toward your setup of Missed-Call Rescue, Lead Engine, or AI Front Desk.

What does the setup fee pay for?

Setup pays for building it for your business. We write the texts and questions with you, test it with you, and walk your team through it. You approve the wording before anything goes out.

Is there a contract?

Plans are month to month after launch. You can cancel with 30 days' notice. Pay a year up front and get 2 months free.

Are there any other costs?

Business texting has to be registered with the phone carriers. That registration is billed to you at our cost. We handle the paperwork. There is no overage bill for texts. Read more in our plain-English guide to business texting rules.

What about bigger projects?

Work beyond missed calls and leads, like intake, quoting, or reporting, is a Custom Build. Custom Builds start at $3,000 setup plus $500 a month. Not sure what you need? An AI Process Audit is $1,500 for a written plan, credited toward a build.

How do I know if it's worth it for my business?

Use your own numbers. Missed calls per week x close rate x average job value gives the most a week of missed calls could be worth. Our free ROI calculator does the math. For an AI receptionist, see our cost comparison with a part-time receptionist.

Is there a founding-client discount?

Yes. Our first 5 clients get 25% off any setup fee and a locked monthly price for 12 months, in exchange for a short testimonial and permission to share results. That includes Front Desk Lite and Custom Builds, but not the $297 pilot. On Custom Builds, the 25% off never takes the setup fee below $3,000. It does not combine with the $297 pilot credit.

Do prices change by town?

No. Same prices everywhere we work: Idaho Falls, Ammon, Shelley, Rexburg, Rigby, St. Anthony, Pocatello, Chubbuck, Blackfoot, Driggs, and Victor. Teton Peaks AI is based in Idaho Falls.

How do I get a price for my business?

Start with a free 30-minute AI process consultation. If it's a fit, you get a short written plan and a fixed price. You decide.
