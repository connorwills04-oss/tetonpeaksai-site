How electricians can ask for Google reviews

A happy customer may never leave a review if no one asks. A short text, sent at the right time, with one link, is usually enough. Here is how to ask, and what not to do.

Timing

When to ask

  • Same day the job is done. The visit is fresh, and the lights are back on.
  • After the customer is happy. If something is still open, fix it first.
  • Once, maybe one reminder. More than that starts to feel like pressure.

How to ask

A short text with one link

Right after the job

"Thanks for choosing [your shop name] today, [name]. If you have a minute, a Google review helps a small shop like ours a lot: [review link]"

One link, no extra steps.

A gentle reminder

"Hi [name], [your shop name] here. If you haven't had a chance, here's the review link again: [review link]. Thanks either way."

Send once, then stop.

Sample texts, not results from a client. Replace the words in [brackets], and approve the final wording.

Make it a habit

Who asks, and when

  1. 01

    Pick one person

    The tech who did the work, or the office. One owner for the step keeps it from slipping.

  2. 02

    Pick one moment

    Before the truck leaves, or that evening when the invoice goes out.

  3. 03

    Keep a simple list

    Note who was asked and when, so no one gets asked twice.

Example

Asking at the end of a job

A tech finishes swapping a breaker and tests the circuits with the homeowner. Before leaving, the tech says a text with a review link is coming. That evening, the office sends it with the invoice. The homeowner leaves a review that night.

A hypothetical example, not a client story.

Stay within the rules

What Google's review rules don't allow

  • Don't offer discounts, gifts, or money for reviews.
  • Don't ask only the customers you think are happy and skip the rest. Ask everyone the same way.
  • Don't write reviews for your own business or ask staff to.
  • Rules change. Check Google's current review policies for your Business Profile.

Your review link

Where to get the link

Your Google Business Profile has a way to share a link to your review form. Use that link in your texts so customers land in the right place.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

When should an electrician ask for a Google review?

The same day the job is done, once the customer is happy. Send one short text with your review link, and at most one reminder.

Can I offer a discount for a review?

No. Google's review rules don't allow offering money, discounts, or gifts for reviews. Check Google's current policies for details.

Should I only ask customers I think are happy?

No. Google's rules don't allow picking only happy customers to ask. Ask everyone the same way.

Will the AI reply to reviews without asking me?

No. Review replies are drafts until you OK them.
