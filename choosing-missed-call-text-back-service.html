Test it

Test it before you trust it

  1. 01

    Call from another phone

    Let it ring out and hang up. A text should arrive with your approved wording.

  2. 02

    Reply to the text

    Check that your team gets an alert and can see the reply.

  3. 03

    Reply STOP

    Check that the texts stop. Then reply with START or the provider's opt-in word if you want to keep testing.

  4. 04

    Try after hours

    Do the same test at night or on a weekend.

Watch for

Red flags

  • You can't see or change the text wording.
  • No clear answer on who registers your texting.
  • A long contract before you've seen it work.
  • Promises of a set number of jobs or results. No one can promise that.

Fit

FAQ

Questions owners ask

What should I look for in a missed-call text back service?

Check that customers keep calling your same number, that you approve the wording, who handles texting registration, that STOP and HELP are built in, how you get alerts, any text limits, and how you cancel.

Do I need a new phone number for missed-call text back?

No new software for you, and customers keep calling your same business number. Behind the scenes, we add a texting number for your business. Callers get the text from that number.

How many texts are included in Missed-Call Rescue?

Up to 500 texts a month. We let you know before you reach the limit, and there is no overage bill.
