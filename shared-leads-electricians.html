The problem

Why shared leads are hard for small shops

Several shops, one job

The customer may hear from more than one electrician. Ask each service how many pros can get the same request.

You pay either way

On many services you pay for the lead, whether or not you win the job. Check the terms of yours.

You are on a job

The lead shows up while your hands are in a panel. By the time you call back, they may have picked someone.

Respond fast

A first reply you can send from anywhere

  1. 01

    Reply with a question, not a pitch

    Ask what the job is and where it is. A question gets an answer. A pitch gets ignored.

  2. 02

    Ask for a photo

    Photos help you give a real next step. See how to ask for photos before a visit.

  3. 03

    Offer two times

    Give a clear choice for a call or a visit. See booking electrical jobs by text.

  4. 04

    Follow up once or twice

    Then stop. See estimate follow-up texts.

Spend wisely

Pause leads when no one can answer

People who worked with these services say contractors get burned when leads keep coming while no one can respond. If your service lets you pause or cap spending, use it on days you are slammed or away.

Track cost per booked job, not cost per lead. A cheap lead you never answered cost you the most.

Long term

Build leads you don't have to share

  • Your Google Business Profile, with honest hours and real job photos. See local search and AI visibility.
  • Reviews from real customers, asked for the right way. See how to ask for Google reviews.
  • Past customers, who already trust you. See past-customer reactivation.
  • Your own phone line, with every missed caller getting a reply. See missed-call text back for electricians.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

Are Angi and Thumbtack leads shared with other electricians?

They can be. These services may let one customer reach more than one pro for the same job. Ask the service how many pros can get the same request, and read the current terms.

How fast should I respond to a shared lead?

As fast as you can with something useful. A short reply with a question or a photo request beats a long pitch hours later.

How do I judge if a lead service is worth it?

Track cost per booked job, not cost per lead. Count the leads you could not answer in time too.

Does missed-call text back work with lead services?

It replies to people who call your business number and get no answer. Leads that only arrive inside a lead service app follow that service's own steps.
