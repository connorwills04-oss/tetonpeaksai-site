Why it matters in Idaho Falls

Idaho Falls averages about 15 nights a year below zero, and January lows average about 12 degrees, per NOAA. Cold mornings bring dead batteries, no-starts, and tow-ins. In summer, US 20 carries travelers north from Idaho Falls toward Yellowstone, and some of them will need a shop on the way.

A no-start on 17th Street

Say a driver's pickup won't turn over in a parking lot on 17th Street on a below-zero morning. She calls your shop while your service writer is at the counter with another customer.
