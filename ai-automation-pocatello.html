Pocatello

Business in Pocatello

Pocatello is home to about 58,800 people, per 2025 Census estimates. Idaho State University is Bannock County's largest employer, and its fall 2025 enrollment of 13,933 was its highest since 2012. Portneuf Medical Center, Idaho Central Credit Union, and the Pocatello School District are also major employers. Interstates 15 and 86 meet at the north edge of town. Whether a caller is a student, a family, or a driver passing through, a quick text back keeps a missed call from turning into a lost customer.

Older homes, cold winters

About two thirds of Pocatello homes were built before 1980, per the Census, and January lows average about 17 degrees. Cold snaps bring furnace and pipe calls.

The ISU calendar

Idaho State's fall 2026 classes began August 24, and finals run December 14 to 18. Student calls rise and fall with that calendar.
