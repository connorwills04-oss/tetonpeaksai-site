How electricians should reply to reviews, good and bad

People can read your replies, not just your reviews. A short, calm reply shows someone is paying attention. Here is how to answer each kind of review.

By type

Three kinds of reviews, three kinds of replies

Good review

Thank them by name, mention the job if it fits, and keep it to two sentences.

Mixed review

Thank them, own the part that was fair, and say what you're doing about it.

Bad review

Stay calm. Say you want to make it right, and give a way to reach you directly. Then call them.

Sample replies

Replies you can start from

Good review

"Thanks, [name]. Glad we could get your kitchen circuits sorted out. We appreciate you choosing us."

Short and specific.

Mixed review

"Thanks for the feedback, [name]. You're right that we were late, and we're sorry. We've changed how we schedule morning calls."

Only say what is true.

Bad review

"We're sorry this visit didn't go well, [name]. We want to make it right. Please call or text us at [your number] and ask for [owner name]."

Take the details offline.

Sample texts, not results from a client. Replace the words in [brackets], and approve the final wording.

Leave it out

What not to put in a public reply

  • Don't argue or list everything the customer did wrong.
  • Don't share job details, prices, or anything private about the customer.
  • Don't offer a refund or discount in public. Handle that one on one.
  • If a review looks fake or breaks Google's rules, you can flag it to Google instead of fighting it in the replies.

Timing

Keep up without living in your inbox

  • Check once or twice a week. Set a reminder. Replies a few days later are fine.
  • One person owns it. The owner, or someone the owner trusts to speak for the shop.
  • Keep a few starting points. Save two or three replies you like, then change them to fit each review.
  • Cool off on bad ones. Write the reply, wait an hour, and read it again before posting.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

Should an electrician reply to every review?

Replying to every review, good and bad, shows customers someone is paying attention. Keep replies short.

How should I reply to a bad review?

Stay calm, say you want to make it right, and give a way to reach you directly. Don't argue or share job details in public.

What if a review is fake?

If a review looks fake or breaks Google's rules, you can flag it to Google. Check Google's current review policies for how.

Will the AI reply to reviews without asking me?

No. Review replies are drafts until you OK them.
