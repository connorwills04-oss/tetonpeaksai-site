How to book electrical jobs by text

Phone tag wastes days. Texting lets customers pick a time when it suits them. Here is a simple way to book service calls and estimate visits by text, from the first reply to the day of the job.

The flow

Five steps from reply to booked

  1. 01

    Offer two times

    Two clear choices get faster answers than "When works for you?"

  2. 02

    Confirm the details

    Repeat back the day, the time window, and the address.

  3. 03

    Say what to expect

    Who is coming, roughly how long, and anything they should do first, like clearing a path to the panel.

  4. 04

    Remind the day before

    A short reminder with an easy way to reschedule.

  5. 05

    Text when you're on the way

    Optional, but customers like knowing when to expect you.

Sample texts

Booking texts you can start from

Offer times

"Hi [name], this is [your shop name]. We can come Tuesday 8 to 10 AM or Wednesday 1 to 3 PM. Which works better?"

Two choices, clear windows.

Confirm

"You're set for Tuesday, 8 to 10 AM, at [address]. Please make sure we can get to the panel. Reply here with any questions."

Repeat the address so mistakes show up early.

Reminder

"Reminder from [your shop name]: we'll see you tomorrow, 8 to 10 AM. Need a different time? Just reply."

Makes rescheduling easy instead of a no-show.

Sample texts, not results from a client. Replace the words in [brackets], and approve the final wording.

Booking links

When a booking link helps, and when it doesn't

A booking link lets customers pick a time on their own, any time of day. It works well for estimate visits and simple service calls with set time slots. It works less well when the job needs questions first, or when your day changes a lot. You can use both: a link for estimates, and texts for service calls.

Lead Engine includes a booking link. See Lead Engine. If you want calls answered and booked any time, see AI Front Desk.

A booked visit with no phone tag

A homeowner asks about adding outlets in a garage. Your office texts two times. The homeowner picks Wednesday afternoon. A confirmation goes out with the address. The day before, a reminder goes out, and they reply that a neighbor will let you in.

How do I book service calls by text?

Offer two specific times, confirm the day, time window, and address, say what to expect, and send a reminder the day before with an easy way to reschedule.
