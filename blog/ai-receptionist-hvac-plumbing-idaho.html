What's the difference between missed-call text back and an AI receptionist?

Missed-call text back sends the caller a text after you miss the call. An AI receptionist answers the call. It asks the questions you choose and books appointments 24/7.

Why does it matter in an Idaho winter?

January lows in Idaho Falls average about 12 degrees. When a furnace goes out on a cold night, a homeowner may call the next company on the list. A fast answer tells them you got the call.

Fall is busy too. The first fall freeze in Idaho Falls usually comes around September 20, so blowout requests and furnace calls stack up while your crew is out.

What would it ask a homeowner with no heat?

You choose the questions. For example: their address, what's wrong, whether the heat is out, and the best number to reach them. Urgent calls stand out from routine ones, so you know which to call back first.

This is a hypothetical example, not a client story.

What if the AI says something wrong?

You set the rules. Texts follow wording you approve. Review replies are drafts until you OK them.

Do customers have to call a new number?

No. Customers keep calling your same business number. Behind the scenes, we add a new number for your business, and the calls we handle forward to it.

Will it replace my office person?

No. A great office person builds relationships an AI can't. The AI covers the calls nobody is free for: lunch, after hours, weekends, and when the line is already busy. We compare the costs in AI receptionist vs. a part-time receptionist.
