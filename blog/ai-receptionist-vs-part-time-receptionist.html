Should you hire a part-time receptionist or try an AI receptionist? Here is the cost side, using public wage data.

What a receptionist costs in Idaho

The median wage for receptionists in Idaho is $17.70 an hour (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2025). At 20 hours a week, that's about $1,534 a month in wages. Full-time is about $3,068 a month. Payroll taxes, benefits, and training come on top.

What our AI Front Desk costs

$497 a month plus $2,497 setup. It answers calls 24/7, asks the questions you choose, and books appointments.

It's not either-or

A great front desk person builds relationships an AI can't. The AI covers the calls nobody is free for: lunch, after hours, weekends, and when the line is already busy. Some owners will want both.

Sources: BLS, Receptionists and Information Clerks, Idaho median hourly wage series.
