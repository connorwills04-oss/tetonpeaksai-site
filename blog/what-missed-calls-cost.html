What do missed calls cost your business?

A simple way to put a dollar number on missed calls, using your own call log, close rate, and average job value.

Home / Blog / What do missed calls cost your business?

Most owners know they miss calls. Few know what those calls are worth. Here is a simple way to find out with your own numbers.

The formula

Missed calls per week x close rate x average job value = revenue at stake each week. Multiply by 52 and divide by 12 for a monthly number.

A made-up example

Say a shop misses 5 calls a week, closes 30% of callers it talks to, and an average job is $400. That's 5 x 0.30 x $400 = $600 a week, or about $2,600 a month. These numbers are only an example. Yours will be different.

Why it's a ceiling, not a promise

Some missed calls are spam, existing customers, or people who call back. So treat the number as the most you could be losing. Even a small slice of it can pay for a simple fix.
