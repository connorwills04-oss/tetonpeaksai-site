Why it matters in Rexburg

BYU-Idaho approved housing contracts end at 6 p.m. on set days, like December 18, 2026 for fall. Before they leave, students have to pass a white glove clean check, and the final check has no recheck. Landlords then turn units over before winter check-in on January 4, 2027. In the yard, the first fall freeze usually comes around September 24, so sprinkler blowouts and fall cleanups crowd into the same weeks.
