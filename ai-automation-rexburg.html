Business in Rexburg

Rexburg grew from about 25,500 people in 2010 to about 40,300 in 2025, per the Census. BYU-Idaho is Madison County's largest employer, and the university estimates about 22,000 of its students lived in Rexburg in fall 2025. BYU-Idaho runs fall, winter, and spring semesters, so students arrive and leave several times a year. Only about half of residents lived in the same home a year earlier. That means a steady flow of first-time callers. A quick text back answers them before they move on to the next search result.
