Blackfoot

Business in Blackfoot and Bingham County

Blackfoot is the county seat of Bingham County, the top potato-growing county in the country. Visit Idaho calls it the Potato Capital of the World, and it is home to the Idaho Potato Museum. Bingham Memorial Hospital and the Shoshone-Bannock Tribes are among the county's largest employers, per the Idaho Department of Labor, and Basic American Foods runs a potato plant in Blackfoot. Manufacturing had about 2,400 jobs in the county in 2025. When the owner is on a job or in the field, a quick text back keeps a missed call from turning into a lost customer.

Local timing

When calls get missed in Blackfoot

Potato harvest

Most Idaho potatoes are dug in the last two weeks of September and the first two weeks of October, per the Idaho Potato Commission. Everyone is busy at once, and calls get missed.

Fair week

The Eastern Idaho State Fair runs nine days in Blackfoot, starting Labor Day weekend. It drew 253,906 guests in 2026. Nearby restaurants, shops, and services get extra calls that week.
