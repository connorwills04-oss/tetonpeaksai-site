Idaho small business

Idaho runs on small business

Idaho has about 207,700 small businesses, per the U.S. Small Business Administration. That is 99.2% of all businesses in the state. They employ about 386,000 people, 56% of Idaho employees. Most have no employees at all, and about 41,900 more have 1 to 19. In a business that size, the owner may also be the receptionist and the person on the job.
