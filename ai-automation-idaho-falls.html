When calls get missed in Idaho Falls

Winter cold snaps

January lows average about 12 degrees, and a typical winter brings about 15 nights below zero. Furnace, frozen pipe, and no-start calls tend to land on the same mornings.

Early starts at INL

More than 2,500 INL employees ride lab buses out to the desert Site and back each day. Many of those customers can only call a business before the bus or after they get home.

Sprinkler blowouts

The City of Idaho Falls reminds homeowners to blow out sprinklers before a hard freeze, and the first fall freeze usually comes around September 20. Fall calls stack up fast.
