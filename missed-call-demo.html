Simulation only. This page sends nothing. Sample numbers use the fictional 555-01xx range. Notification wording above is an illustration; the real look depends on the phone and the Quo app.
  • Built on Quo auto-replies, which text a set message when a call is missed or a voicemail is left, with separate messages for business hours and after hours.
  • Quo sends an auto-reply at most once every 3 hours per caller, and skips it if you recently called or texted that person.
  • The text comes from the business's Quo texting number, a local number in the business's name. It is not the main line unless that line is moved to Quo.
  • Texting goes live only after carrier registration is approved. Replies of STOP are handled by Quo automatically.
  • The owner reads and answers replies in the Quo app (phone, desktop, or web). Email alerts are optional.
