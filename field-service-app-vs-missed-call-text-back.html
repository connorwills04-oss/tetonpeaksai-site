Check first

Questions to ask your field service app

  • Does it text back missed calls to my main business number, or only to a separate number?
  • Does it text on every missed call, including nights and weekends?
  • Can I set and approve the wording?
  • Who handles texting registration, and is mine approved?
  • Who sees the replies, and do I get an alert on my phone?
  • Is it included in my plan, or an add-on?

Common gaps

Where shops still miss calls

Feature is there, but off

It was never set up, or texting registration was never finished. Check your settings and ask your app's support.

Calls go to a cell phone

If calls ring a cell phone that isn't tied to the app, the app never sees the missed call.

Replies go nowhere

Texts go out, but replies land in an inbox no one checks in the field.

No after-hours plan

The app texts during business hours only, or no one follows up on night replies.

Decide

How to decide

  1. 01

    If your app covers it

    Turn it on, test it from another phone, and you're done. We'll tell you that on the free call.

  2. 02

    If it covers part of it

    Fix the gap, like where calls ring or who sees replies.

  3. 03

    If it doesn't

    A separate missed-call text back can work alongside your app. We check how your calls ring first. Customers keep calling your same business number.

Test it

A two-minute test

Call your business number from a phone that isn't yours, let it ring out, and hang up. Did a text arrive? Did someone on your team get an alert? Reply to the text. Did the reply reach someone who can call back? Try it once during the day and once after hours.
