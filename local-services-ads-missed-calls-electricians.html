Google Local Services Ads and missed calls: what electricians should know

If you run Local Services Ads, a call you miss can still count as a lead you pay for. Here is how Google describes it, in plain words, and what to do so the leads you pay for get an answer.

How Google counts it

What Google's help page says about missed calls

  • You pay for valid leads. A call you answer, a text or email, a voicemail, or a booking request can count.
  • A missed call during your business hours counts when the caller stays on the line more than 20 seconds.
  • If the call goes to voicemail or a greeting and the caller stays on more than 20 seconds, Google treats it as connected, even if they leave no message.
  • If your phone menu asks callers to press a key, the 20 seconds start after the first key press. No key press, no charge.
  • "Business hours" are the hours in your Google Business Profile.
  • More calls or messages from the same number or email within 15 days are not charged again.

Source

Check the current rules yourself

This summary comes from Google's Local Services help page, "How leads work." Google changes these rules, so read the current page before you change your setup. It also explains lead credits and the feedback survey for poor leads.

What to do

Make every paid lead hear back

  1. 01

    Match your hours to reality

    If no one can answer at 7 AM, don't list 7 AM in your Business Profile hours. Missed calls in listed hours can be charged.

  2. 02

    Reply to every missed caller fast

    A paid lead that hears nothing calls the next shop. A text back reaches them right away. See missed-call text back for electricians.

  3. 03

    Use the follow-up window

    Follow-ups with the same customer within 15 days are not charged again, so a callback or text costs nothing extra.

  4. 04

    Rate the bad leads

    Use the feedback survey in your leads inbox for spam or wrong-trade calls, as Google describes.

  5. 05

    Count what you miss

    Check how many ad calls you missed last week. See how to count your missed calls.

If your missed calls forward somewhere

Some shops forward unanswered calls to voicemail, an answering service, or a text back number. Under Google's rules, a call that reaches a voicemail or greeting and stays on past 20 seconds is treated as connected. That is one more reason to make sure every one of those callers gets a reply.
