The problem

Too much busywork. Not enough hands.

You're on a job, with a customer, or closing up. The phone rings and you can't get it. That caller doesn't leave a message. They call someone else.

The same thing happens with web forms and Facebook messages that sit for hours, and with happy customers who never get asked for a review. None of it is hard. There's just no one free to do it.

Big companies have IT teams to build automations. Most local businesses don't have the time or budget for that. That's where we come in.

How we got the wage numbers: the median wage for receptionists in Idaho is $17.70 an hour (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025, occupation 43-4171). Part-time: $17.70 x 20 hours x 52 weeks / 12 = $1,534.00 a month. Full-time: $17.70 x 40 x 52 / 12 = $3,068.00. Wages only. BLS source, Idaho series.
