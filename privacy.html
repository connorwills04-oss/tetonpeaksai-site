Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Home / Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Prooformance LLC, doing business as Teton Peaks AI ("we," "us"), collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are an Idaho limited liability company. It covers this website (tetonpeaksai.com), calls and texts with our business number, email with us, and our services.

Information we collect

Forms on this site

Our AI scorecard form asks for your name, business name, email, and phone number, along with your scorecard answers.

Booking a call

When you book a consultation, you use Calendly, a scheduling service. We receive the details you enter there, such as your name, email, and the time you picked.

Calls

When you call us, we receive your phone number, the time and length of the call, and any voicemail you leave.

Texts

When you text us or we text you, we keep your phone number, the messages, and records of when you opted in or opted out.

Email

When you email us, we keep your email address and what you send.

Technical information

Our website host and Google Analytics may receive your IP address and basic browser information when you visit. This site uses Google Analytics to understand visits. We do not sell your data.

How we use information

  • To reply to you and answer your questions.
  • To send your scorecard results and follow up on your request.
  • To schedule consultations and send reminders.
  • To provide the services you sign up for, and to bill for them.
  • To send texts you have agreed to receive.
  • To keep records of consent and opt-outs.
  • To keep our systems secure and to follow the law.

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only so they can do that work for us. These include:

  • Phone and text messaging providers.
  • Our form service (Formspree), which receives scorecard form entries.
  • Our scheduling service (Calendly).
  • Email and website hosting providers.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

Text messaging privacy

We do not share mobile phone numbers or text messaging opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

The sharing described above does not include text messaging opt-in data and consent. We do not share that information with any third party.

You can stop our texts at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply.

Information we handle for our clients

When we set up tools for a client business, such as missed-call text back, we handle that business's customer information on its behalf. The client business decides how its customers' information is used, and its own privacy policy applies. We do not use that information for our own marketing.

How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the reasons in this policy, or as long as the law requires. We keep opt-in and opt-out records so we can honor your choices.

Security

We use reasonable steps to protect your information, such as limiting who can access it and using services with account security. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without permission.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you by emailing us.
  • Stop texts at any time by replying STOP, or by telling us by phone or email.
  • Stop emails from us by replying and asking us to stop.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under the law.

Children

This site and our services are for businesses and adults. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child gave us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will post the new version here and change the effective date.
