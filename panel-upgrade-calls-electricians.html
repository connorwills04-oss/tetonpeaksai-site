Why they call

Common reasons, and what each one means for you

Reason they giveWhat to askHow urgent it usually is
Adding an EV charger or heat pumpWhat are they adding, and when? Is there room in the panel now?Tied to their project date
Home sale or inspection reportWhat does the report say? When is closing?Often a hard deadline
Insurance or lender askedWhat exactly did they ask for, and by when?Often a deadline
Breakers trip or the panel is oldHow often does it trip, and what is running when it does?Ask about heat, smell, or sparks first
Remodel or additionWho is the contractor, and what is the schedule?Tied to the build schedule

These are common patterns, not rules. Your people decide what is urgent.

First call

Questions that save a trip

  • Why are you looking at a panel upgrade now?
  • How old is the home, and do you know the panel's age or brand?
  • Can you text a photo of the panel with the door open, and the label inside the door?
  • Is there a date you need it done by?
  • Do you own the home?

Safety first

When the call is not a quote

If a caller mentions heat, a burning smell, or sparks at the panel, that is not a quote request. Your shop decides how to handle it. You can tell callers to call 911 if there is fire or immediate danger. A text after a missed call does not diagnose anything.

After the quote

Deadline jobs need a different follow-up

A buyer with a closing date or an inspection deadline needs fast, clear answers: when you can start, what permit or utility steps apply, and what they need to do. A buyer planning an EV charger or heat pump may take longer and compare shops. Follow up on a slower schedule. See estimate follow-up texts.

Example

Two panel calls in one day

In the morning, a seller calls with an inspection report and a closing date in three weeks. You book a visit for the next day. In the afternoon, a homeowner asks about a panel for a future heat pump. You ask for photos and a project date, send a quote by text, and set a follow-up for the week after.

A hypothetical example, not a client story.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

What should I ask when someone calls about a panel upgrade?

Ask why they want it now, how old the home and panel are, whether they have a deadline, and whether they own the home. Ask for photos of the panel and its label.

Why does the reason for a panel upgrade matter?

It tells you how urgent the job is and how to follow up. A home sale deadline needs fast answers. A future EV charger or heat pump may take longer to decide.

What if a caller says the panel is hot or smells like burning?

That is not a quote request. Your shop decides how to handle it. You can tell callers to call 911 if there is fire or immediate danger.

Can missed-call text back tell callers which panel jobs are urgent?

No. A text after a missed call is not dispatch and it does not diagnose anything. Your people decide what is urgent and who goes out.
