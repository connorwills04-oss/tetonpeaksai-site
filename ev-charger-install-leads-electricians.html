EV charger install leads: what to ask before you quote

EV charger calls often start with "How much to put in a charger?" The honest answer depends on the panel, the distance, and the charger. Here is how to collect what you need without a trip out for every call.

Why these calls are different

The price depends on things the caller can tell you

A home EV charger is usually a Level 2 charger on a 240-volt circuit. Whether that is a simple job or a bigger one comes down to a few things: room in the panel, the service size, how far the parking spot is from the panel, and whether the wall is finished or open.

The caller usually knows some of this, or can take a photo of it. A few short questions up front save you a wasted trip and help you give a real quote faster.

Intake

Questions to ask every EV charger caller

  • Do you already have the charger? If yes, ask for the make and model. If not, ask if they want your advice on one.
  • Where do you park? Garage, carport, or driveway. Inside or outside makes a difference.
  • Where is your electrical panel? Garage, basement, or outside wall, and roughly how far from where you park.
  • How old is the house, and has the panel been replaced? This hints at whether a panel upgrade might come up.
  • Is anything else big being added? A heat pump, hot tub, or addition changes the load picture.
  • Do you own the home? Renters may need the owner to OK the work.

Good to know

Permits, rebates, and panels

  • Permit and inspection rules vary by city and county. Tell the customer if a permit is part of the job.
  • Some utilities and states offer charger rebates. Point the customer to their utility to check. Don't promise one.
  • If the panel doesn't have room, the job may turn into a panel upgrade. See panel upgrade calls: what to ask first.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

What should an electrician ask before quoting an EV charger install?

Ask if they have the charger and which one, where they park, where the panel is and how far away, how old the panel is, and whether anything else big is being added. Ask for photos of the panel and the parking spot.

Can I quote an EV charger install from photos?

Often you can give a closer estimate from photos, or decide if a site visit is needed. Some jobs still need a visit before a firm price. That is your call.

What if the panel doesn't have room for a charger?

Then the job may include a panel upgrade or other changes. It is better to learn that from photos before the visit than at the visit.
