Example

What a missed caller gets

A homeowner calls at 6:40 PM about a breaker that keeps tripping. You're finishing another service call. Right away they get a text: "Hi, this is [your shop name]. Sorry we missed your call. What's going on, and what's the address? We'll call you back soon." You get an alert with their number and their reply.

A hypothetical example, not a client story.

Good to know

Text back is not dispatch.

  • A text after a missed call does not diagnose anything or decide what is an emergency. Your people make those calls.
  • You approve the wording, so you choose what the text tells callers.
  • Texts follow carrier rules. People can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. See business texting rules.
