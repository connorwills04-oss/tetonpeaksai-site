What does the text say?

You approve the wording. A common example: "Sorry we missed you. How can we help?" The caller can reply by text and tell you what they need.

Do I need a new phone number or new software?

No new software for you, and customers keep calling your same business number. Behind the scenes, we add a texting number for your business. Callers get the text from that number.

What happens when the caller replies?

You get an alert, so the lead doesn't go to someone else. You or your team reply when free, between jobs or after the customer in front of you.

Does it work after hours and on weekends?

Yes. A text goes out on every missed call, any time. That includes nights, weekends, and the busy season.

Is it legal to text people who called me?

Business texts have to be registered with the phone carriers (A2P 10DLC). Every text program should honor STOP to opt out and HELP for help. We handle the registration and build in STOP and HELP. This is general information, not legal advice. See our guide to business texting rules.

How long does setup take?

If it includes texting, that starts once the phone carriers approve it. That can take from a couple of days to a few weeks.
