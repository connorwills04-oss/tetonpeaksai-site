Before you sign up

Questions to ask any provider

  • What happens to an emergency call? Who decides it is urgent, and how does it reach a person on my team?
  • Can I approve the script or the wording callers hear and read?
  • What does it cost each month, and what counts as extra?
  • How do messages and leads reach me: text, email, or an app?
  • Do customers keep calling my same business number?
  • How do I cancel, and how much notice do you need?

Straight answer

When an answering service is the better pick

If your shop takes emergency calls at night and you want a live person on every one of them, an answering service or an on-call phone may fit better than AI. An AI receptionist does not diagnose anything or decide what is an emergency. Your people make those calls. On the free call, we'll tell you if we're not the right fit.

Example

How one shop might decide

A two-truck residential shop misses calls most afternoons while both electricians are on jobs. Most callers want a quote or a time, not an emergency visit. The owner starts with missed-call text back to see how many callers reply. If calls keep piling up, the next step is an AI receptionist that answers and books. If the shop starts taking emergency calls at night, a live answering service or an on-call phone may make more sense for those.

A hypothetical example, not a client story.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

What is the difference between an AI receptionist and an answering service?

An answering service uses live people who answer your calls for a fee. An AI receptionist uses AI to answer, ask the questions you choose, and book appointments. Our AI receptionist is the AI Front Desk plan, and it answers 24/7.

Which is better for an electrical contractor?

It depends on your calls. If you want a live person on every emergency call, an answering service may fit better. If you want calls answered and booked any time, look at an AI receptionist. If you mostly need missed callers not to go to the next shop, start with missed-call text back.

How much does AI Front Desk cost?

$497 a month plus $2,497 setup. It's month to month after launch, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

How much does an answering service cost?

It depends on the service and the plan. Ask each provider what is included and what counts as extra. We don't sell answering services.

Can an AI receptionist handle electrical emergencies?

It does not diagnose anything or decide what is an emergency. Your people make those calls. How it handles your calls, including getting calls to you, is set up with you.

Do I need new software or a new phone number?

No new software for you, and customers keep calling your same business number. Behind the scenes, we add a new number for your business, and the calls we handle forward to it.
