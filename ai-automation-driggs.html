Driggs

Teton Valley business

Driggs is the county seat of Teton County, Idaho, about 12 miles from Grand Targhee Resort. The county's top industries include construction, lodging, and food service, and almost 90% of local companies have 20 or fewer employees. Business swings with the seasons. In 2023, county lodging sales ran about $4.4 million to $6.7 million a month from July through October, and under $2 million a month in November and December. Many owners also struggle to hire. When you are short-staffed in peak season, calls get missed. A quick text back keeps that guest or customer from booking somewhere else.

Local timing

When calls get missed in Teton Valley

Ski season

Grand Targhee, about 12 miles from Driggs, plans to open for the 2026-27 winter season on November 20. Lodging, rental, and restaurant calls pick up with it.

Long, cold winters

Driggs averages about 24 nights a year below zero, and January lows average about 9 degrees, per NOAA. The first fall freeze usually comes around September 9.

Over the pass

About 3,950 Teton County residents work outside the county, many in Jackson, per the county's 2024 economic plan. When your crew is driving Teton Pass, the phone doesn't always get answered.
